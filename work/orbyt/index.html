Orbyt

Creator · Feb 2025 to present

Orbyt is a short-form video app built around communities, with looping video and its own look and feel. It runs on the AT Protocol, the open network behind Bluesky, so people own what they make: their profile, posts and follows are stored with their own account, not on Orbyt's servers. I designed, built and launched it, and it's in beta on iOS and the web.

iOS and web Product, brand, launch getorbyt.com ↗

The idea

Most video apps are built for broadcasting to strangers. I wanted something closer to a group chat for a shared interest: a place for /pets, /loops or /archive where the people posting and the people watching overlap.

Orbyt has its own profiles, follows, notifications and communities, and it connects to Bluesky the way Vine connected to Twitter. Anyone with a Bluesky account can join in one tap, find the friends they already follow, and share posts back to the wider network.

What I built

  • Communities. Each one has its own feed, members and owner, with member-led moderation.
  • Two feeds. Following, and Your mix.
  • Looping video. Every clip plays on a clean loop, with no stutter at the restart.
  • Creator tools. Audio looping and automatic clip-to-beat editing to cut down editing time.
  • Brand and launch. Name, icon, store screenshots and the web presence.

What it taught me

Orbyt is where I learned how platforms actually work. With Gym Tracker I knew my audience personally: Virginia Tech students with one specific problem. With Orbyt I aimed for mass appeal, and a social video app doesn't grow that way. It needs a niche to start from and the right moment to catch. I plan for both from day one now.

Orbyt is still in active development. If people go looking for an open alternative to today's vertical video apps, one where they own everything they post, it's ready for them.
