Vicino AI

Marketing Intern · Nov 2025 to Feb 2026

Vicino is an early-stage AI startup. When I joined, it was building agents that turn a line of text or an image into a 3D model, for game studios, e-commerce teams and designers. I worked directly with the founder on the brand, the launch, and the people using the product.

Early-stage startup Brand, launch, clients

What I did

  • Brand. Worked with the founder on the mission statement, the brand guide and the white paper.
  • Launch content. Wrote launch posts, one of the company's first LinkedIn posts, and an article for the news page that explained the platform to business buyers.
  • Beta invites. Planned the beta invitation email with the technical team: who got it, when it went out, the template, and checks that every recipient had opted in.
  • Client tracking. Recommended HubSpot and set up its free plan for onboarding and tagging clients.
  • Client support. Was a go-to contact for clients, wrote the user guides and kept them current as features changed, and brought in the engineering lead when a problem was technical.

Making it clear

An early article needed a graphic explaining how the product works: one agent generates a model, and a second reviews it. My first idea was playful, with cats in different-colored jumpsuits playing each role. It was rejected because it didn't explain anything. I replaced it with a plain diagram in the brand style, and the article shipped with it.

The same thing held with clients. When one couldn't get a generated model into Blender, I shared my screen and walked them through the export step by step until they could do it on their own.

What it taught me

At a small company, marketing is everything between the product and the people using it: what you say about it, how they find it, and how they get help. I learned to keep the message tied to what the product does today.

I also learned to match the tool to the team. HubSpot worked for onboarding and tagging, but we never used most of it, and something simpler would have saved setup time.
