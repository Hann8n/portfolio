Old Burying Ground

Eagle Scout project · 2018 to 2019

For my Eagle Scout project I led the restoration of the Old Burying Ground, a historic cemetery in Allentown, New Jersey, with The Allentown Village Initiative (TAVI). We righted and cleaned every gravestone, installed two signs about the site and the people buried there, and added a pamphlet box for visitors.

Allentown, NJ Research, planning, leading a crew Project page on TAVI ↗

How it started

In August 2018 I led a project to landscape part of the cemetery. While we worked I noticed most of the graves couldn't be read. I was looking for an Eagle project, so I researched the site's history and got in touch with TAVI.

Scope

The first plan was one sign with information on each grave and a virtual tour to go with it. My troop and TAVI pushed me to take on more, so it grew to two signs and a restoration of the stones themselves. I worked with TAVI members Martha Ploshay and Nancy Tindall and town historian Alice Wikoff, and we finished in under a year.
