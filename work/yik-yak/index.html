Yik Yak, Virginia Tech

Campus Ambassador · Feb 2022 to Feb 2024

Yik Yak is an anonymous, location-based app where every campus has its own feed. As the Virginia Tech ambassador I ran the school's Instagram page: picking the posts worth sharing, designing how they looked, and partnering with other campus accounts. The page grew to more than 5.5k followers, and Virginia Tech ranked as the 8th most active herd on the app.

Instagram Content, design, partnerships @yikyakvtech

One format, every day

I built a template that turns a yak into a post in minutes: the Virginia Tech pattern in school orange, the post card, and the vote count. It made the page recognizable in a feed and let me post while a joke was still current.

Timing and partnerships

The best posts landed on moments the whole campus shared: game days, finals, graduation. I co-posted with Red Cup VT and the Radford Yik Yak page to reach their followers, and added stories and short video to keep the page from feeling like a repost account.

On campus

I worked with Yik Yak on a sign-up event at Squires, the student center. The Yik Yak van came to campus, and every student who signed up or showed us the app got a pair of Yik Yak socks.

2023 recap

To close out 2023 I made a ten-slide recap for the page, in the style of a year-end wrapped post: the herd's most active day, the yak of the year, the most upvoted yak, and the running stories students followed all semester, like Movie Guy and Union Wi-Fi vs. Tornado.

Other campuses

Ambassadors at three other schools hired me to design the look of their pages, each built on their own school colors and pattern.

What I took from it

The posts that spread were ones students already agreed with. The job was to spot them fast, make them look like they belonged to the school, and post at the moment they would land.
